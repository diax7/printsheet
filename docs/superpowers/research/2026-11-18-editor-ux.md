# Editor UX research (2026-11-18)

Input for the editor redesign (owner's request: Canva/Photoshop-like, every action next to the
selected design, nothing missing on a phone). "Consensus" means at least two real products;
single-source and vendor claims are marked.

## 1. Contextual action bar on the selection

- Canva desktop: floating toolbar above or below the selected element, with Delete and Lock
  (https://www.canva.com/help/add-elements/, https://www.canva.com/help/lock-and-unlock-elements/).
  Order "lock, duplicate, delete, More" is from third-party write-ups, indicative only.
- Adobe Express: contextual toolbar on the artwork; Lock under its three-dot menu
  (https://helpx.adobe.com/express/web/invite-collaborate/object-locking.html).
- Photoshop on the web: Contextual Task Bar under the selection
  (https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/desktop/get-started/learn-the-basics/boost-workflows-with-the-contextual-task-bar.html).
- Differ: VistaCreate fixed panel above the artboard; Figma right sidebar; Drip gang sheet
  builder toolbar above the canvas plus right-click Delete
  (https://docs.dripapps.com/drip-sheets/for-customers/settings-within-build-a-gang-sheet).
- A critique of Canva iOS: Lock and layer order buried in secondary menus
  (https://ixd.prattsi.org/2026/02/design-critique-canva-ios-app/).
- Recommendation: Duplicate, Delete, Lock, Rotate 90, then "..." (flip, layer order, align).
  Icons with tooltips on PC, icon plus short label on phone. Not verified whether Canva's bar is
  icon-only.

## 2. Mobile editor layout

- Canvas full screen; a bottom bar that changes with the selection; more options in a
  swipeable bar or overflow; panels as bottom sheets. Canva (bottom bar per the Pratt critique;
  long-press and "More" confirmed at https://www.canva.com/help/glow-up/), Photoshop mobile
  (bottom tool row, pinch zoom, https://www.wired.com/story/everything-you-can-do-in-the-photoshop-mobile-app/),
  Drip phone builder (bottom bar Select / Add Image / Settings), Antigro 3.0 (bottom sheets, sheet
  size and fill % in the header; vendor claim, https://gangsheetbuilder.com/).
- Anti-pattern: Drip hides a setting (Image Margin) on narrow screens.
- Figma mobile and Kittl do not edit designs on a phone.
- Touch model (consistent outside the list): one finger on an object moves it, on empty canvas
  pans; pinch zooms; tap empty space deselects; long-press opens a menu (PostRoll
  https://help.postroll.app/boards/on-a-phone/, MolDraw). Antigro: press-and-hold to move tiny
  items whose handles are hidden.
- Bottom sheets: non-blocking, with a drag handle, about half height at most
  (https://m3.material.io/components/bottom-sheets/guidelines). Touch targets 44 pt (Apple) /
  48 dp (Material).
- Scroll vs canvas: touch-action none on the canvas only, inside a non-scrolling 100dvh shell
  (MDN touch-action). iOS Safari overscroll and pull-to-refresh need a real-device test
  (WebKit bug 275947).

## 3. Desktop layout

- Left rail for adding content and layers, selection properties on the right or in a bar above
  the canvas, top bar for file, undo/redo, export, zoom at a corner: Canva, Figma UI3
  (https://www.figma.com/blog/making-the-move-to-ui3-a-guide-to-figmas-next-chapter/), Kittl,
  Drip (left rail + toolbar with AutoNest, Fill Sheet, Duplicate, rotate, align, undo/redo,
  https://docs.dripapps.com/drip-sheets/for-customers/sticker-sheets), Gangio.
- Complaints (Adobe Express community): tools hidden behind menus, panels covering the selected
  item.

## 4. Gang sheet builders

- Length (and price) always visible, often live; fill % in the header (Antigro, vendor claim).
- Auto arrange as a prominent action; Auto Build: size and copies per design, then pack (Drip,
  https://docs.dripapps.com/drip-sheets/for-customers/how-auto-build-works); manual + Duplicate +
  Auto-Arrange All (https://formulatedprints.com/pages/how-to-build-a-gang-sheet); gap choice and
  auto-rotation (Crafters Vinyl Express).
- Quantity: a copies stepper per design; Fill Sheet repeats one design.
- Quality: outline per image coloured by DPI, overlap outlines, a pre-checkout quality check with
  "Print anyway" (DTF Now, Drip). Thresholds differ by shop.
- Shopify reviews praise auto build and mobile support; end-user complaints found only on shop
  help pages (uploads lost, white boxes, stretched art, long sheets easier on desktop).

## 5. Shortcuts and gestures (consensus: Canva, Adobe Express, VistaCreate, Kittl, Figma)

Delete/Backspace; Ctrl+D (also Alt+drag); arrows with Shift for a bigger step; Ctrl+Z, redo as
Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y; Shift+click and marquee; Ctrl+scroll zoom; Space+drag pan; pinch zoom
and long-press menu on phones; a "?" shortcut list (Drip). No consensus on a Lock shortcut.

## Ranked changes for PrintSheet (phone first)

1. Floating action bar on the selected design (flips below near the top edge): Duplicate,
   Delete, Lock, Rotate 90, "...".
2. Phone: fixed bottom bar at thumb height (Add, Arrange, Undo/Redo, Fit, More); New, Open,
   Save, background and settings in the More sheet: overflow, never hide.
3. Size, rotation and align in a non-modal bottom sheet (about 40%) so the design stays visible.
4. Touch: drag design to move, drag empty canvas to pan, pinch zoom, tap empty to deselect,
   long-press menu; test on a real iPhone.
5. 44 to 48 px touch targets, bigger handles on touch, press-and-hold to move tiny designs.
6. Film length (and fill %) always visible in the top bar, phone too.
7. Arrange prominent, with a copies stepper per design, Fill Sheet and a visible gap setting.
8. Outlines by DPI and for overlaps/off-sheet designs; a pre-export checklist.
9. PC shortcuts: add Space+drag pan, Alt+drag duplicate, accept Ctrl+Y, a "?" list.
10. PC top bar grouped File | Undo/Redo | Zoom | Export; right panel for the selection, sheet
    settings otherwise; snap guides on.

## Not verified

VistaCreate and Adobe Express phone toolbars; whether Canva's bar is icon-only; Canva's
pan-versus-select on phones; iOS Safari overscroll behaviour (needs a real device).

# Easier editor on phone and PC: plan (rev 2)

Roadmap item 2 (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-18-roadmap.md`). Research:
`docs/superpowers/research/2026-11-18-editor-ux.md`. Rev 2 answers CTO plan review 1 (81/100).

Owner's picks (2026-11-18): **phone**, the bottom bar swaps to the selected design's actions;
**PC**, a floating action bar at the selected design, **below** it (he chose below after seeing
that the size label and Fabric's rotate handle sit above the selection).

Owner's words: "like Photoshop or Canva ... if I want to duplicate, I want to delete, I should not
scroll back down ... for the phone there are some functions and buttons that don't show ... make
it usable and easy for all users."

## Goals

1. **PC floating action bar**, in an overlay layer that is a sibling of the scroller. Placed
   **below** the selection's on-screen box (scene bounding box through `viewportTransform` and
   the canvas element's client rectangle), flipped above when there is no room below, clamped
   inside the stage horizontally, hidden when the selection is scrolled fully out of view and
   while dragging, scaling or rotating. Recomputed on `after:render`, scroll, zoom, resize and
   store change. One design: Duplicate, Delete, Lock/Unlock, Rotate 90°, Flip, More. Several:
   Duplicate, Delete, Lock all, Align, More. More (`role="menu"`): flip vertically, bring forward
   / send backward / to front / to back, Add copies, Move onto the sheet / a sheet (when
   flagged), Arrange selection. Right-click on a design opens the same menu. Toolbar semantics:
   `role="toolbar"`, roving tabindex with arrow keys, reached with Alt+F10 or Tab from the stage;
   appearing never steals focus; Escape closes the menu and returns focus to More.
2. **Phone (max-width 820 px): one screen, no page scroll.** App shell `height: 100vh;
   height: 100dvh`, `overscroll-behavior: none` on html and body, `viewport-fit=cover` with
   `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` padding on the bottom bar; user zoom stays allowed.
   - Top bar: logo, film metres, Undo, Redo, Export.
   - Bottom bar, nothing selected: Add, Designs, Arrange, Sheet, More (New, Open, Save, Zoom
     in, Zoom out, Fit, Background, Grid, About).
   - Bottom bar, one design selected: Duplicate, Delete, Lock, Rotate, Size, More, and a close
     (deselect) button. Several selected: Duplicate, Delete, Lock all, Align, More, close.
   - Designs, Sheet, Size and More open as **bottom sheets** (`role="dialog"`,
     `aria-modal="false"`, labelled; focus moves to the sheet heading on open and back to the bar
     button on close; drag handle with `touch-action: none`; at most about half the screen).
     When a sheet opens over the selected design, the canvas scrolls so the design sits above
     the sheet. A tap on another design keeps the Size sheet open for that design; a tap on
     empty canvas deselects and closes it.
   - **One UI, moved, not copied (M2).** `phone-shell.ts` re-parents the existing DOM on a
     `matchMedia('(max-width: 820px)')` change: `#inspector` into the Size sheet, the designs
     section into the Designs sheet, the sheet settings into the Sheet sheet, the file and view
     buttons into More, and back on PC. Their render code (`mountPanel`, `mountInspector`) does
     not change, so every control keeps its id and exists on both.
   - **Multi-select on the phone (M4):** each row of the Designs sheet gets a checkbox, plus
     "Select all"; the bottom bar then shows the "several" state.
   - **Keyboard over sheets (M3):** inputs at least 16 px on `(pointer: coarse)` (no iOS zoom on
     focus); a `visualViewport` resize listener lifts the open sheet by
     `innerHeight - visualViewport.height`.
3. **Touch on the canvas (M1).** `touch.ts` listens to `pointerdown` in the capture phase on the
   canvas wrapper, for `pointerType === 'touch'` only:
   - a touch on the **active object** (or its handles) goes to Fabric: move, scale, rotate;
   - anything else is stopped (`stopImmediatePropagation`) and handled by `touch.ts`: a tap
     (under 10 px and 300 ms) on a design selects it (`store.setSelection`), on empty canvas
     deselects; a drag pans (`panBy`); a long press (600 ms, cancelled past 8 px) on a design
     opens its More menu;
   - a second finger cancels whatever is running: a "suppress commit" flag stops Fabric's
     `object:modified` from committing the half-moved position, Fabric gets a pointer up, and
     `sync()` puts the object back from the store; the two fingers then pinch (`setZoom` around
     their midpoint) and pan;
   - no marquee selection on touch (multi-select comes from the Designs sheet);
   - `touch-action: none` on the canvas (replacing `allowTouchScrolling`),
     `-webkit-touch-callout: none` and `user-select: none` on the stage, `contextmenu`
     prevented there; Fabric `touchCornerSize` 44 on coarse pointers.
   Dense gang sheets have little empty canvas, so a drag on an unselected design pans: tap to
   select, then drag to move.
4. **PC shortcuts.** New: Space + drag pans (while Space is held: `skipTargetFind`, no
   selection, grab cursor, `preventDefault`; ignored when a button or input has focus); Alt +
   drag duplicates (the copy stays in place, the original moves, one undo step; Alt on a corner
   handle still scales from the centre, as Fabric does); "?" opens the shortcuts list (the list
   leaves the empty inspector). Existing and unchanged: Delete, Ctrl+D, arrows (Shift 1 cm),
   Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z, Ctrl+Y, Ctrl+wheel, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+S, Ctrl+V.
5. **PC top bar grouped**: File (New, Open, Save) | Undo, Redo | Zoom | film metres | Export.
   Below 1100 px the file actions move into an overflow menu instead of disappearing.
6. Touch sizing (44 px buttons and handles) follows `(pointer: coarse)`, so a tablet in PC
   layout gets it too.

## Interim state

Phase 2 ships before the gestures: `allowTouchScrolling` stays on until Phase 3, so a finger
still scrolls the canvas; zoom is in More until pinch arrives; the existing phone swipe tests
keep passing through Phase 2 and are replaced in Phase 3.

## Non-goals

No change to the model, Arrange, export or file formats; no new runtime dependency (gestures by
hand on pointer events; Fabric stays the canvas).

## Constraints

- New code in its own modules: `src/editor/action-bar.ts` (with pure placement maths in a small
  testable function), `src/editor/phone-shell.ts`, `src/editor/bottom-sheet.ts`,
  `src/editor/touch.ts`. `mountCanvas` gains only thin `CanvasView` methods: `panBy(dx, dy)`,
  `targetAt(clientX, clientY)`, `cancelGesture()`, `screenBox(ids)`; `mountPanel` and
  `mountInspector` do not grow.
- The intent of the earlier phone fixes stays: the whole sheet reachable, the page never jumping
  (`revealInOwnBox` for the Designs sheet's own scroll), no empty help panel on the phone.
- Spec: amend goal 18 and the non-goals (phones become a supported target, iPhone Safari and
  Android Chrome) in Phase 2.

## Tests

- Unit: bar placement (below, flip above, clamp, hidden off screen), gesture classification
  (tap, drag, long press, second finger), sheet lift maths.
- Playwright PC (1440 × 900): bar appears below, flips above at the bottom edge, hides while
  dragging, every bar and menu action works, right-click menu, keyboard (Alt+F10, arrows,
  Escape), Space-pan, Alt-drag duplicate, "?".
- Playwright phone (390 × 844, isMobile, hasTouch, Chromium CDP touch): no page scroll; the
  bottom bar's three states; every sheet opens and closes with focus handling; the inventory
  walk; Designs checkboxes; pinch zoom; one-finger pan on empty canvas and on an unselected
  design (the design does not move); a pinch begun on the selected design leaves it where it
  was; tap selects; long press opens More.
- **Owner's iPhone checklist** (WebKit cannot run here), after Phase 3:
  - a drag that starts on a design you have not chosen pans and does not choose it;
  - drag the chosen design twice in a row: both drags move it;
  - a two-finger pinch that starts with one finger on the chosen design leaves it where it was;
  - one finger pans on empty canvas and over a design that is not chosen (the design stays);
  - pinch zooms around the fingers, and the page itself never zooms;
  - long press (about half a second) on a design chooses it and opens its menu, with no
    Safari callout or text selection;
  - tap a design, then drag it to move it (one Undo puts it back);
  - grab a corner of a chosen design to resize it, and the round handle above it to turn it,
    with a finger;
  - type a width in the Size sheet with the keyboard up; pull down at the top (no reload);
    rotate the phone (landscape uses the computer layout);
  - the gestures decide on whichever event of a touch comes first (Chrome sends pointer events
    first, Safari may send touch events first); if any item above fails on the iPhone, note
    which, as it may be Safari's event order.

## Phases

1. PC floating action bar, More and right-click menu, shortcuts, grouped top bar.
2. Phone shell: top bar, bottom bar (three states), bottom sheets with the moved DOM,
   multi-select, keyboard lift, spec amendment.
3. Touch gestures (capture handler, pan, pinch, long press, cancel-and-sync), replacing touch
   scrolling.

Each phase: tests first, gates by exit code, screenshots at 390 × 844 and 1440 × 900 checked by
eye, CTO review, then deploy.

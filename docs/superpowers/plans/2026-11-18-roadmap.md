# Roadmap after smart Arrange (2026-11-18)

Agreed with the owner on 2026-11-18, in this order. Each item gets its own plan, CTO review,
tests and a separate deploy.

1. **CMYK Compact.** Done 2026-11-18 (CTO GO 92). A four-channel CMYK JPEG for the compact PDF
   (DCTDecode, DeviceCMYK, APP14 transform 0, inverted samples, Decode 1 0 1 0 1 0 1 0), written
   by our own encoder; soft mask lossless. Checked with two independent PDF engines against the
   lossless CMYK file: pdf.js (mean colour difference under 6) and MuPDF (CMYK plates within
   about 1 level: C 1.2, M 0.9, Y 1.1, K 1.5). On the owner's 3.03 m job: 67.2 MB lossless,
   40.6 MB compact (RGB compact: 16.7 MB). Not yet checked in a print shop's RIP.
2. **Easier editor, phone and PC.** From `docs/superpowers/research/2026-11-18-editor-ux.md`:
   a floating action bar on the selected design (Duplicate, Delete, Lock, Rotate, more), a phone
   bottom bar with nothing hidden (New, Open, Save, background under More), properties in a
   non-blocking bottom sheet, touch gestures (drag, pan, pinch, long-press). Mockups with A/B
   choices go to the owner before building.
3. **Vector files.** SVG (native), PDF and AI saved with PDF compatibility (rendered with
   pdf.js). EPS is refused with a message to re-save as SVG or PDF (a PostScript engine is too
   large and its licence too restrictive). The original vector is kept and drawn at the placed
   size and full print resolution on screen and in every export, so vectors are never blurry;
   their row says "Vector, sharp at any size" instead of a DPI level. Every design row shows a
   file-type badge (SVG, PDF, AI, PSD, PNG, JPG, WEBP).
   **Photoshop files** (owner, 2026-11-19), in the same item: a PSD opens a list of its layers
   with previews to pick from (visible layers ticked, hidden ones unticked, a folder added as one
   merged design or opened to pick its layers), plus "Whole image as one design". A layer with
   effects (stroke, shadow, glow) is added without them and its row says so; the whole image
   keeps them, as Photoshop saved it.
4. **Quantity per design.** A "x N" counter with + and - on each design row; Arrange packs them.
5. **Size presets and recent sheets.** One-tap print sizes (adult front, pocket, kids, sleeve,
   own presets) and a list of past sheets in the browser to reopen for reorders.
6. **Photo Editor**, a screen of its own for one image, opened from any design or from a
   warning when an added image has a background ("Open it in the Photo Editor?"). Done replaces
   the design's image on the sheet (one undo). Version 1, all chosen by the owner:
   - background colour removal (eyedropper on any colour, tolerance, from the edges or
     everywhere);
   - automatic removal for many images at once (owner, 2026-11-19): after images are added,
     the app finds each one with no transparency whose border is mostly one colour (white for
     one, black for another, any colour), says "N images have a background. Remove it?", and on
     Yes removes each image's own colour from the edges inward with a soft edge and no fringe,
     in one undo. An image whose background is a picture or a gradient is listed as not
     removed, with a way to open it in the Photo Editor; the manual way stays for every image;
   - edge cleanup (shrink 1 to 2 px, remove fringe) and an erase / restore brush;
   - crop, rotate, recolour (one colour, invert);
   - outline (sticker border), adjust (brightness, contrast, saturation), plain 2x upscale.
   Later, after a discussion with the owner: halftone (his own plan), AI upscale and AI
   background removal (both need an AI model: a large on-device download or an online service,
   a privacy and size choice for him to make).

Earlier suggestions not chosen for now: thin-line warning, cut helpers (cut lines, name tags),
ruler test strip, Arabic interface.
